01 / The floating halo

A signature, not a signboard.

A suspended elliptical ribbon frames the brand and brings the five divisions into a single visual gesture. Concealed warm LEDs define its inner edge.

02 / The sample gallery

Small objects. Big stories.

Five individually lit display chambers showcase physical print, signage, craft, spatial and fabrication samples. Digital services live in the central touch console.

03 / The material story

Quiet luxury. Real materials.

Graphite powder-coated steel structure, satin metallic cladding, engineered stone top and removable clear display enclosures. Finish selections are conceptual.

04 / The build logic

Designed to come apart.

Five demountable modules, a removable overhead assembly, rear service access and segregated low-voltage cable routes. Structural fixing and stability require engineering review.
